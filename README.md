# Distributed Energy Resource Data Catalog
This catalog description outlines the temporal and geographic availability of various datasets related to energy and Distributed Energy Resources (DERs):

###Monthly and Annual Data: 
Available at the national level and for individual states, covering both current data and projections.

###Projections (Monthly, up to 2050): 
National-level data on future energy trends and DER integration.

###State-Level Data (Annual and Monthly): 
Data available for individual states, with national aggregates included in a comprehensive spreadsheet.

###Annual Data: 
National and state-level data available for specific years, such as 2018, providing snapshots of energy consumption and DER performance.

###Data Accessibility: 
Some datasets are accessible at varying temporal resolutions, including annual, monthly, daily, and hourly intervals.

###Coordinate-Based Data Retrieval: 
Certain datasets allow users to look up available data at specific geographic coordinates, with results returned based on the closest matching data.

###City-Level Data: 
Detailed energy datasets for cities, particularly within California, offering annual and real-time data at granular levels (e.g., county or sub-region).

###Wind Turbine Data: 
Annual and specific-day datasets for regions surrounding wind turbines, providing insights into wind energy performance.

###Private Data: 
Some datasets, though noted in the catalog, remain private and are accessible only to restricted users, though sample data may be available.
This catalog offers a range of temporal (daily, monthly, annual) and geographic (national, state, county, city, sub-region) granularity, making it suitable for detailed analysis of DERs across the U.S. and other specific regions.
